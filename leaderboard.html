How we calculate

Profit from the trades in the window

We read every swap in the stock-token pools on Uniswap and credit it to the wallet whose token balance changed. Profit is what a wallet received from selling, minus what it paid for buying, plus the tokens it still holds from those trades at today’s on-chain price.

Only trades inside the window count, so tokens bought before it don’t distort the result. Dollar values use the pool price at the time of the trade; ETH is converted at the current rate.

What’s left out

Real traders, not routing

Arbitrage bots and routers end each transaction without a position, so they drop out. Wallets averaging more than four trades an hour are marked as bots and hidden unless you switch them on. Wallets with under $250 of trades aren’t ranked.

Wallets are anonymous addresses, and a person can use several. This is on-chain data, not financial advice.
