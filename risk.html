Transparent by design

How the risk score works

The score runs from 0 to 100, where higher is safer. Low risk needs at least 75 points and an audit; medium needs 50. It’s a rule of thumb, not a guarantee.

What goes into the score

25 pts
Audit: at least one public audit report listed on DefiLlama.
25 pts
Pool TVL on a log scale, full marks at $100M.
20 pts
Protocol age, full marks at two years.
15 pts
Liquidity: a single asset with no impermanent loss scores full marks; pools with IL score 0.
15 pts
How much of the APY is real interest rather than reward tokens.

An audited protocol can still get hacked, and a high score doesn’t make a pool safe. Use it to compare, not to decide on its own.

Independent ranking

Nobody pays for a place in the table

Table order is set only by the column you sort by. If a protocol ever pays for visibility, it will appear in a separate, clearly marked box, never inside the table.

Where the data comes from

On-chain, via DefiLlama

Pools, APY and TVL come from DefiLlama Yields, which reads them directly on-chain. Audits and protocol age come from DefiLlama’s protocol list.
