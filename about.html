About Tidewatch

Same market. A clearer view.

Tidewatch makes on-chain yield on Robinhood Chain easy to compare, so you can see what your capital could earn and what risk comes with it, in one place.

What we stand for

Three principles

01

Independent

No protocol pays for a place in the table. Rankings follow the column you sort by, nothing else.

02

Transparent

The risk score is fully documented, and every number links back to public on-chain data.

03

Non-custodial

Tidewatch is read-only. No wallet connection, no deposits, no sign-up.

Data

Where the numbers come from

Pools, APY and TVL come from DefiLlama Yields, which reads them on-chain. Audit links and launch dates come from DefiLlama’s protocol list. You can check any transaction on the Robinhood Chain explorer.

Business model

How Tidewatch stays free

Tidewatch is free today and has no paid placements. If we add sponsored spots later, they’ll be clearly labelled and kept outside the ranking table.

Questions or a pool we’re missing? Reach us on X @usetidewatch.
