Stock yield

Your shares, working harder

A stock token tracks the underlying share, and dividends go to both. The difference is what you can earn on top by lending the token out or providing liquidity.

How stock tokens work

One token, one share

On Robinhood Chain, stock tokens are standard ERC-20 tokens. Each started as one underlying share, and a built-in multiplier adjusts for dividends and splits without changing your balance.

Prices come from a Chainlink feed per stock, so lending markets can value them 24/7.

What to watch

Lending vs. liquidity

Lending a stock token keeps your exposure simple: you earn interest and keep the share. Providing liquidity usually pays more, but you risk impermanent loss if the share price moves a lot.
