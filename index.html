How the risk score works

The score runs from 0 to 100, where higher is safer. Low risk needs at least 75 points and an audit; medium needs 50. It’s a rule of thumb, not a guarantee, and an audited protocol can still get hacked.

25 pts
Audit: at least one public audit report listed on DefiLlama.
25 pts
Pool TVL on a log scale, full marks at $100M.
20 pts
Protocol age, full marks at two years.
15 pts
Liquidity: a single asset with no impermanent loss scores full marks; pools with IL score 0.
15 pts
How much of the APY is real interest rather than reward tokens.

Table order is set only by the column you sort by. If a protocol ever pays for visibility, it will appear in a separate, clearly marked box, never inside the table. Data: DefiLlama Yields, which reads pools directly on-chain.
